What the bot is

A hobby project that posts a daily digest of Borderlands 4, Palworld, and Diablo IV news, answers /news commands, and announces new SHiFT codes. I built it in my spare time, with more enthusiasm than quality assurance.

No promises

The bot is provided as is, with no warranty of any kind. Specifically:

  • The summaries are written by an AI. They're usually right and occasionally confident about things that aren't true, which puts them roughly on par with me. Check the linked sources before you win or lose an argument over one.
  • "Rumor" means rumor. Please don't plan your vacation around one.
  • SHiFT codes are passed along as found. They may be expired, region-locked, fake, or already redeemed by someone faster than you. The bot doesn't check, and neither do I.
  • The bot may be down, late, wrong, or quietly broken for days before anyone notices. It will break. It has before.
