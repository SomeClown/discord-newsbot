What the bot is

A hobby project, now open to any Discord server. It keeps a catalog of popular games (Borderlands 4, Palworld, Diablo IV and a dozen or so others), and a server admin picks which ones to follow and where each one posts. Each morning at the time they choose, it posts a digest of recent news for those games. It also answers /news commands and can announce new SHiFT codes. I built it in my spare time, with more enthusiasm than quality assurance.

What's free

The free tier is a headline digest (a plain list of links, with no AI in it) plus SHiFT code alerts. Summaries written by an AI are not part of it; I switch those on myself for a few servers I choose to cover, on my own API keys. That's a courtesy to those servers and not something you can buy, expect, or be promised. There is no paid tier today. If one ever shows up, it'll arrive with its own terms.

No promises

The bot is provided as is, with no warranty of any kind and no service level. Specifically:

  • Where there are summaries, an AI wrote them. They're usually right and occasionally confident about things that aren't true, which puts them roughly on par with me. Check the linked sources before you win or lose an argument over one.
  • Headlines come from public feeds and searches and are passed along as found. A headline is not an endorsement, and a "rumor" is a rumor. Please don't plan your vacation around one.
  • SHiFT codes are passed along as found. They may be expired, region-locked, fake, or already redeemed by someone faster than you. The bot doesn't check, and neither do I.
  • The bot may be down, late, wrong, or quietly broken for days before anyone notices. It will break. It has before.
  • I can change what the bot does, which games it covers and how it behaves, or shut it down, whenever I like, possibly out of embarrassment.

Servers and admins

Setting the bot up takes the Manage Server permission, and the admin who does it is responsible for what they set. That means which channels the bot posts in, who can read those channels, and who gets pinged. The SHiFT alerts can ping @everyone or a role if an admin chooses that, and the bot will do exactly what it was told. If you don't want that, leave the ping on "none", which is the default.

I may remove the bot from any server, or refuse to join one, at any time and for any reason, abuse of the bot or of Discord's rules included. Server admins can remove it whenever they like, and doing so deletes that server's settings (see the Privacy Policy for the details and the one small exception).
